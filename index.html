Flow

Rules built into the system
  • Only speaker, sponsor and exhibitor pages that organisers publish. No logins, no attendee databases, no LinkedIn scraping.
  • Personal data is hidden until a reviewer enters the passcode. Without it the public view shows initials and role level only. Company, contact data, enrichment and the HubSpot payload are not sent to the browser. Before approval a lead holds only name, title, company and source link.
  • Enrichment and the HubSpot payload start only after approval. The payload is never sent from this build. The note and opener are templates for now, and an LLM step plugs into the same place. Nothing is sent to anyone.
  • Every review is written to an audit log with the reviewer's name. Failed steps go to a dead-letter queue and show on the Health tab.
  • Sales Navigator is a manual link per lead. Nothing scrapes it.
  • Outreach is out of scope for this build. Germany: no cold email, a LinkedIn note from a person first, and no call until a concrete topical reason is recorded (UWG section 7). UK: checked against PECR and TPS. US: CAN-SPAM.
Scoring a lead

A fast-and-frugal tree with named exits. A lead with no buyer function or from a vendor is dropped. Below manager is tier C. A title that owns the invoice, payment, order or purchasing process (AP, AR, billing, order management, shared services, purchasing) is tier A as the champion. A senior person with a second named buyer at the same event is tier A as the economic buyer. The rest are tier B or C. Leads from an event hosted by a competitor, today Radiance, are capped at tier B and flagged. Each card shows the path taken, so you can overrule it.
